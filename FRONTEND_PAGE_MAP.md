## 2. Portal 1 — Customer Web Portal (`(customer)`)

### 2.1 Navigation Structure
- **Global Sticky Header (`CustomerHeader`)**:
  - **Left**: `goodly` logo (links to `/`) + `AddressSelectorTrigger` (`Delivering to [City/Address] ▾` $\rightarrow$ opens `AddressPickerModal`).
  - **Center**: Context-aware Search Bar (global search on `/` and `/search`; switches to in-menu filter with global fallback on `/restaurants/[slug]`).
  - **Right**:
    - When unauthenticated: `Sign in` link (`/auth/login`).
    - When authenticated: `Orders` link (`/orders`) + User Avatar menu (`/account/profile`, `/account/addresses`, `/account/favorites`, `/support/complaints`, `Sign out`).
    - Persistent `Your bag [N]` warm gold button (opens `CartDrawer` on non-restaurant pages or scrolls/highlights sticky bag on restaurant page).
- **Global Footer (`CustomerFooter`)**: `goodly` brand mark, `"Good food. Good neighbors. Always."`, support links, and location/care attribution.

## 3. Portal 2 — Outlet Owner & Branch Staff Web Portal (`(restaurant)`)

### 3.1 Multi-Outlet & Granular Branch Staff Navigation Structure (Final Business Decisions §1 & §2)
- **Strict Role Separation**: `Outlet Owner` (`OUTLET_OWNER`) is the restaurant/outlet business owner (distinct from `Platform Admin` and `Customer Care`). A single `Outlet Owner` can own **multiple outlets (branches)** and view consolidated metrics or switch between branches. `Branch Managers` (`BRANCH_MANAGER`) and `Outlet Staff` (`OUTLET_STAFF`) only see their assigned branch(es) and authorized navigation tabs according to their granular permissions (`outlet:orders:manage`, `outlet:menu:edit`, `outlet:inventory:toggle`, `outlet:hours:manage`, `outlet:capacity:configure`, `outlet:reports:view`).
- **Left Persistent Operations Sidebar (`RestaurantSidebar`)**:
  - Top: **Multi-Outlet Branch Switcher** (`All Branches [Consolidated]` or specific branch e.g., `Sunday Table — Carroll Gardens` / `Sunday Table — Williamsburg`), Verification Badge, **Live Kitchen Toggle** (`🟢 Accepting Orders` / `🔴 Paused`), and **Active Order Capacity Meter** (`Active Orders: 6 / 12 Max`).
  - Links: `Live Orders (KDS)` (with pulsing unread count badge), `Menu, Schedules & Stock`, `Hours, Capacity & Branch Staff`, `Consolidated Sales & Settlements`, `Add / Onboard New Outlet`.
- **Top Status Bar**: Audio notification toggle (chime on new incoming order), WebSocket connection indicator (`Live Connected`), and 5-minute acceptance SLA indicator.

## 5. Portal 4 — Platform Administration (`PLATFORM_ADMIN`) & Customer Care (`CUSTOMER_CARE`) Web Portal (`(admin)`)

### 5.1 Strict Role Separation & Navigation (`SRS Section 3.4 & 4.11`, Final Business Decisions §1)
- **Clear Role Distinction**:
  - **`PLATFORM_ADMIN`** (`Admin` = Platform Owner / App Owner): Full governance over commission rates (20% initial + calculation basis), minimum cart threshold & small-cart fee, zone & systemwide distance delivery pricing, delivery partner base + distance payout rules, KYC verifications, service zones, promotions, refunds, and system timeouts.
  - **`CUSTOMER_CARE`** (`Customer Care` = Separate Operational Support Role): Restricted operational workspace focused on **5-Minute Restaurant Acceptance Timeout Alerts**, grace-period follow-ups, customer complaints resolution, and **authorized Delivery OTP exception overrides** (with mandatory audit reason). `CUSTOMER_CARE` is strictly blocked from modifying platform commissions, pricing rules, or service zones.
- **Left Navigation Rail (`AdminSidebar`)**: Dynamically locks or hides administrative configuration routes when signed in as `CUSTOMER_CARE`.
